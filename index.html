01

12 golden rules that work for every airdrop

Follow just this section and you'll be ahead of 80% of users. Projects reward real, consistent users — not one-day farmers.

01
Consistency, not bursts

Instead of 50 transactions in one day, be active 3–5 days every week. Log activity across at least 4–8 different weeks and 2–3 different months.

02
Diversify your actions

Don't repeat a single action: Bridge + Swap + Add Liquidity + Stake + Mint NFT + Deploy + Vote. Variety = real user.

03
Use sensible volumes

Tiny, identical amounts every time (e.g. always 0.001) look suspicious. Vary your amounts and keep some balance on the network.

04
Age your wallet

A wallet with months of history, activity across multiple networks, and a balance is much harder to flag as Sybil.

05
Complete your socials

Follow the official X, Discord, and Telegram. Be active in Discord, claim roles, and complete Galxe / Zealy / Layer3 tasks.

06
Give feedback & report bugs

On testnets, a bug report with a screenshot and your wallet address is one of the strongest signals — and most people skip it.

07
Never use bots or scripts

Identical time gaps, identical gas, and repeating patterns are easy to detect. Do everything manually.

08
Don't move funds between your wallets

If you have multiple wallets, don't fund them all from one exchange/source and don't transfer between them. You'll be detected as a cluster.

09
Don't get caught by the snapshot

Snapshots are usually taken without warning. Don't suddenly drain your balance or stake, and keep farming until TGE.

10
Build an on-chain identity

An ENS / domain name, SBT NFTs, DAO votes, and a Gitcoin Passport all raise your wallet's trust score.

11
Official links only

90% of airdrop scams come from fake Claim links. Never share your seed phrase, and only claim from the official site.

12
Keep records

For each project, save the date of activity, TX hashes, and screenshots. You'll need them for feedback forms or eligibility appeals.

Important questions & warnings

Are airdrops for these 8 projects guaranteed?

No. Never count on any project unless it has officially announced one itself. This guide maximizes your chance if an airdrop happens — but some projects may never issue a token, may do it late, or may use different criteria. Budget your time and money accordingly.

How many wallets should I farm with?

For most users, one clean main wallet with real history is the best choice. Multi-accounting raises the Sybil risk of losing all your wallets at once, and in recent airdrops its upside has become smaller than its risk.

How much money do I need?

The testnets (Titan, Flop, the Fhenix test side, and Buzzchain) are nearly free. Mainnet / DeFi parts like Dow need gas and a little liquidity. Start small, and never put in money you need.

How will I know when a Claim opens?

Only from the project's official X and Discord. Any Claim link you see in a DM, an unknown Telegram, or a comment is almost certainly phishing. Verify the news on two official channels first, then act.
