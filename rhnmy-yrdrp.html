Confidential smart contracts on Ethereum, powered by FHE

Official site @fhenix

Its CoFHE coprocessor is now live on Ethereum and Arbitrum mainnet, and the community campaign runs through the OnlyFhens quest hub.

What to do

  1. Open the OnlyFhens quest hub at onlyfhens.fhenix.io and connect your X account.
  2. Follow @fhenix on X and complete the follow and repost quests.
  3. Do the post / tag quests — publish the required posts mentioning Fhenix to earn credits.
  4. Keep an eye out for new quest drops and CoFHE mainnet activity, and use the same wallet and X account throughout.

Eligibility

  • OnlyFhens quests completed and credits earned
  • X account connected, following @fhenix, reposts done
  • Consistent activity over several weeks — not one burst
  • One wallet and one X account only — no multi-accounting
Tip: Credits are the score here. New quests appear over time, so check the hub regularly instead of doing everything in a single day.
